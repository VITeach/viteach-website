# Charvi Upreti

> _“Taking the first step, from awkward beginnings to lasting bonds, VITeach revealed the immeasurable rewards of connection and the power of making a difference.”_  
> — **Charvi Upreti**

VITeach was the first club I applied to after being drawn by its mission of educating underprivileged students. With both my parents as teachers, I felt a strong personal connection to the club's vision. An immediate sense of belonging led me to volunteer actively, stepping outside my comfort zone. Recognizing it as the perfect fit, I joined VITeach as my **FFCS club**.

I then pursued the **Junior Core Committee (JCC)** role, gaining valuable hands-on experience across various departments. The JCC selection process was rigorous, and the news of my selection brought indescribable joy, as it was my first time applying for an important position in a club.

As a JCC member, particularly within the **design team**, I contributed to organizing key VITeach events like **Escape Verse** and **Mini Expo**. These experiences honed my teamwork, communication, and task management skills. Balancing club work with academics was certainly challenging, but my passion for the mission and the supportive VITeach environment made it manageable. I genuinely enjoyed what I was doing, and the club's efficient internal structure certainly streamlined our work.

My involvement spanned multiple departments, offering diverse experiences:

- **Design Team:** Created event posters and social media content
- **School Team:** Developed PPTs and worksheets for students, culminating in an unforgettable school visit
- **Online Outreach:** Conducted one-on-one sessions teaching basic Python commands, which was incredibly fulfilling knowing I made a positive impact

My first school visit remains a vivid memory. I remember being super awkward with the kids at first, but seeing how eager they were to connect and interact, I gradually became comfortable, and the bond formed very naturally. This experience taught me that people are open to connection; sometimes, we simply need to initiate it by stepping beyond our comfort zones.

One of my proudest moments was speaking at a **Sustainable Development Goals (SDG)** session, where we discussed the impact of small actions—such as cycling to school—on the environment. We also explored how these goals can improve education in government schools, followed by a detailed analysis of specific goals like:

- Poverty eradication
- Gender equality
- Quality education

Another highlight was organizing the **Jenga game at the Mini Expo**, which turned out to be a resounding success and, above all, a fun experience.

Challenges such as last-minute poster creation and unexpected hurdles were overcome through teamwork. Along the way, I formed lasting bonds with incredible individuals, creating memories I will cherish for life.

The end of my tenure was bittersweet. It was deeply gratifying to have contributed meaningfully to a club that felt like home, yet nostalgic, as VITeach had become an integral part of my journey. It was the place of many first-time experiences for me and has left a lasting impact—both perceptible and imperceptible.

VITeach instilled valuable skills such as **adaptability, leadership, communication, and teamwork**, which I will carry forward. Observing the club's growth fills me with immense pride for having been a part of it.

To newcomers, my advice is:

> _“Explore diverse departments, discover your passions, and learn along the way. Every effort adds up, and the memories, experiences, and most importantly, the people, make it all worthwhile.”_

For me, VITeach was not just about organizing events or creating content—it was about finding a family within a club, making a difference one small step at a time, and cherishing the bonds that made every challenge worthwhile.
