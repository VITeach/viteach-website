# Canon Event Unlocked: Kalyanasundaram’s Story

> **“மணிமுத்துகள் ஆழியிலே பதைந்து மறைந்து கொண்டிருக்கும் போதிலும்,  
> அந்த மணியின் அழகை அறிந்து பாராட்டியவரின் மனதில் அந்த அழகு சிறக்கிறது.”**  
>
> *(Even though gems lie hidden deep within the earth, their beauty shines brightest in the hearts of those who recognize and appreciate them.)*  
>
> — **Bharathiyar**

One such gem hidden deep within the garden of **VITeach** is **Kalyanasundaram** — the club’s former **Technical Lead** — whose beauty shines through the smiles of the children he worked for.

He joined VITeach for a simple reason — it welcomes all helping hands aboard. No interviews, no questions asked. Sounds simple, right? But for Kalyanasundaram, this was a chance to join a club that didn’t focus on grades or previous experience — it focused on a volunteer’s **potential to grow and contribute**.

VITeach, in his words, is a place that trusts its volunteers with its vision — something rare in today’s world. That trust in his ability to give back to society was the spark that started his journey.

> “I joined VITeach without knowing anything, especially the technical side,”  
he admits, humbly.

And thus, it began.

VITeach is where most of his **character development** happened — yes, real *main character energy* right here! And what’s so wrong with that? Aren’t we all NPCs in someone else’s life?

Starting with no technical experience, he didn’t just gain coding skills — he gained **confidence, responsibility, and leadership**. (A *canon event*, I guess? Still waiting and working for mine!) The journey from basic HTML to advanced React web development was gradual, but each step came with learning how to collaborate, coordinate with his team, and juggle responsibilities.

---

Being a Technical Lead in VITeach wasn’t just about managing the technical infrastructure — it was also about managing people.

Coordinating with design and content leads, he ensured the website was not only functional but also engaging. Beyond that, he was responsible for the club’s entire web presence — monitoring domain renewals, fixing broken links, and reviewing code before it went live.

But of course, every journey has its ups and downs.

His story included some challenging moments, especially during **2020** (we all know the story). The shift to remote work due to the COVID-19 pandemic brought its own set of challenges, particularly in keeping the team connected and maintaining the club’s vibrant energy.

> “During the first five months of my term, coordination was tough.  
> We had to rethink how to engage everyone and keep the spirit of the club alive,  
> which was a big change from being physically together,”  
he recalls.

As a Technical Lead in a non-technical club, the challenge was even more intense.

In video games, you know you're on the right track when challenges keep coming your way. Life’s pretty much the same. Challenges are inevitable — but what makes a game interesting is how we, as players, find ways to overcome them.

Kalyanasundaram faced his toughest level when the pandemic hit. But instead of backing down, he and his team got to work.

> “We had regular meetings, brainstormed ideas, and worked together to find solutions,”  
he says.

The real challenge was figuring out how a social club could stay active when collaborative workshops weren’t an option anymore. They had to think outside the box — designing creative, child-friendly events that aligned with the club’s mission.

---

> “Before joining VITeach, I just focused on academics.  
> After joining, I realized there’s so much more to learn.  
> The experience not only shaped my technical skills, but also helped me grow as a person.”

It taught him teamwork, communication, and leadership. He even got into the **Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador Program** — something he never thought was possible before.

For Kalyanasundaram, VITeach wasn’t just a stepping stone in his career; it was a **defining chapter** in his life — a place where he learned the value of trust, communication, and leadership that he continues to carry forward.

> “It’s not just about being on time,”  
> he continues,  
> “it’s about recognizing progress, even if the task isn’t fully done.  
> As a lead, I learned the importance of punctuality, even without strict deadlines.”

It’s a lesson he’ll carry with him moving forward.

---

For anyone reading Kalyanasundaram’s journey and wondering, *“When will something like this happen to me?”* — take a moment to reflect on this:

He didn’t wait for the perfect moment.  
Even when he had no experience, he didn’t think, *“I don’t deserve this.”*  
He took action.

The truth is, **canon events aren’t just fictional — they happen in real life.**  
All we have to do is put in the effort to make them happen.

**Be the canon event.**  
A little discipline, perseverance, and punctuality — just like Kalyanasundaram — can change everything.
