# Janice Thomas  
**By Swetha**

Janice Thomas, the former **Outreach Lead of VITeach**, recently shared her thoughts and experiences about her involvement with the club. From her motivations for joining to her favourite memories and the lessons she learned as both a lead and a volunteer, Janice's insights offer a unique perspective on the importance of teaching, learning, and community service.

Janice joined the **Outreach Department** because she wanted to be part of a volunteer-based club focused on education and community service. She was drawn to the club's collaborative and welcoming nature, where members could learn not only from each other but also from the children they taught. She has always loved interacting with kids, and the joy of introducing them to new concepts has been a constant source of inspiration for her.

Through her experience with the Outreach Department, she gained valuable **organizational, collaborative, and leadership skills**. She believes that teaching children is one of the best ways to reinforce one's own understanding of concepts. She also strongly believes that if you can’t explain something to a child, you may not have fully understood it yourself. This approach to learning has given Janice a deeper appreciation for the importance of clear communication and effective teaching.

She shared many fond memories of her time with the Outreach Department, including a day-long event called **EscapeVerse** and a **virtual Python session during the pandemic**, where she and others volunteered to teach children from the slums of Mumbai. These experiences, she says, not only helped her develop new skills but also strengthened her bonds with fellow club volunteers and the children.

By interacting with Janice, I have learned that sharing our knowledge and experiences with others allows us to gain new perspectives and develop skills that benefit us throughout life. Her humility and eagerness to learn at all times are qualities that stood out to me throughout the interview. I hope to embody these same qualities while being part of this incredible community filled with people who radiate kindness and passion.

Janice's story truly highlights the profound impact that social service can have — not only on society, but also on the individual.
