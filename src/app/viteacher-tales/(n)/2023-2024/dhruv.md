# VITeacher TALES – DHRUV KUCHAL

Today on **VITeacher Tales**, we're featuring **Dhruv Kuchal** (Ex-Content Lead) — the guy you can always spot with his black Sony headset and a shirt that’s always perfectly tucked in.

I’m **Deepesh Sai K** from the VITeach Content Team, and I had the pleasure of sitting down with Dhruv to chat about his journey with VITeach. Here’s his story, in his own words.

---

### **Q1: How did you come to know about VITeach? And what inspired you to join?**

**Dhruv:**  
I was in my second year, and all my friends were joining or even starting clubs. I had a bit of FOMO, so I asked a friend for suggestions. He pointed me toward VITeach — a volunteer-based club with no entry tests, plus the opportunity to teach kids. That sounded awesome, so I filled out the form, and voilà, my journey with VITeach began!

---

### **Q2: Can you share a moment when you felt, “I made the right decision joining VITeach”?**

**Dhruv:**  
There wasn’t one singular moment; it was more about the vibe. I remember numerous late-night gmeets — starting around 9 PM and stretching into the early hours — where we’d be brainstorming, planning projects, and laughing together. It was during those fun, productive nights that I truly felt, *“Yeah, this is great — I belong here.”*

---

### **Q3: How did interacting with the kids make you feel? Any memorable moments?**

**Dhruv:**  
Interacting with the kids is one of the best parts of VITeach. As a hosteller, my daily life is packed with exams, books, and job hunts, so those school visits are a refreshing escape. The innocence and enthusiasm of the children are contagious.

I vividly remember a day when, while playing games, the kids surrounded us and showered us with high-fives — all at once. It was pure joy (and yes, we even have a video of that moment!).

---

### **Q4: What did you learn from being a part of the VITeach family?**

**Dhruv:**  
I learned a lot — especially the importance of teamwork and communication. Collaborating with people who bring diverse perspectives, managing complex situations, and working together to create something meaningful were all invaluable lessons that have shaped both my personal and professional growth.

---

### **Q5: How did being part of VITeach influence your personal or professional journey?**

**Dhruv:**  
Being at VITeach allowed me to meet incredible people and form lifelong friendships. Those long hours in gmeets, the shared laughs, and the challenges we overcame together — each moment added up, making my experience richer and my journey more memorable.

---

### **Q6: Tell us about your journey to becoming a JCC of VITeach.**

**Dhruv:**  
After joining, I honestly had no clue how clubs worked in college. I had a knack for writing essays in school, so I joined the content team.

Our then Content Head, **Jafrin akka**, handed us an Excel sheet with various tasks and explained that, as a volunteer club, tasks weren’t assigned — they were chosen. I took on a couple of tasks, like the *Har Ghar Tiranga* post, which is where I first truly connected with Jaf akka.

Later, I even teamed up with **Deepesh** (yes, the same person interviewing me now!) on another project. When the JCC forms were circulated, I thought, *“Why not?”* I gave it a shot, shared my passion for writing, and — lo and behold — I was selected as one of the Content JCCs alongside **Abhinay** and **Deepesh**.

---

### **Q7: Can you give us a quote that sums up your experience at VITeach?**

**Dhruv:**  
> *“Perseverance is not a long race; it is many short ones, one after another.”*

---

### **Q8: Anything else you'd like to share for the blog?**

**Dhruv:**  
Our juniors still haven't sponsored our Goa trip — when's that happening? 😉

---

### **Q9: Tell me more about your lead when you were members, and about your successors.**

**Dhruv:**  
**Jafrin akka** was, without a doubt, the best OB we could ask for. She guided us with everything — from content matters to life advice. Along with **Abhinay** and **Deepesh**, she made our Content JCC a truly special team.

After Abhinay and I became OBs (Deepesh opted out because he was better suited elsewhere and had other responsibilities), my co-lead was **Lavanya**. Together, we shouldered the responsibility of steering VITeach’s future, balancing content and social media.

When the time came to select our successors, we chose **Joshua** and **Pragnya** after much deliberation. Joshua's flair for content writing and Pragnya’s knack for social media made them the perfect duo to carry forward our legacy. They mesh together seamlessly — much like how Lavanya and I did during our tenure.

---

And there you have it — a glimpse into Dhruv’s incredible journey with VITeach. His story reflects not just the challenges and triumphs of leading a content team, but also the genuine bonds and growth we experience in this family.

With this VITeach Content Team sign-off,  
**we’ll meet you again with another exciting tale.**
