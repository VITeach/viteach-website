# Prisha Sri’s J Tale

*“It's a beautiful thought that we're all striving for a future where this club is no longer necessary.”*

---

## Discovering VITeach

I had some time before my 5 o’clock bus when I heard about an exciting event happening on campus. It turned out to be an introduction event for **VITeach**, and the energy in the room immediately drew me in. The engaging activities combined with the club’s noble mission of teaching students deeply resonated with me.

Curious to learn more, I decided to apply. The interview process was smooth and welcoming, instantly making me feel like part of the VITeach family. The school team felt like an immediate click—it seemed perfect, and I couldn’t wait to be a part of it.

---

## First School Visit & A Moment That Stayed Forever

Before my first school visit, we prepared PPT materials and worksheets for first graders. The visit itself was unplanned and completely different from what I had imagined.

The children were full of energy, their radiant smiles lighting up the room. Their excitement was boundless—they could hardly sit still when I asked them to. Instead, they rushed toward me and settled onto my lap with a kind of trust that spoke volumes. In that moment, I realized how comfortable they felt around us.

We played fun games to match their energy, filling the classroom with laughter and joy. On our way back, the girls collected flowers for me—so many that I could barely hold them all. That small gesture left a huge impact on my heart.

---

## Realizing the Impact of Teaching

I taught the children colors, never imagining how much they would remember. When I returned for another visit, they called me by my name—and my heart melted instantly.

That moment made me realize the true weight of our impact. The worksheets, lessons, and conversations were leaving lasting impressions on young minds. Even if we impacted just a few children, it still meant everything. The love and attention the kids gave us made us feel incredibly special too.

---

## Escape Verse & Becoming a School OB

One of our most memorable campus events was **Escape Verse**. Around 20 of us worked on different parts of the event, each bringing unique ideas and opinions. Despite the differences, we came together quickly and became a family. We shared meals, time, and built bonds that will last a lifetime.

Becoming the **School OB** was a moment of pure excitement for me. As soon as my co-lead and I took on the role, we were eager to brainstorm ideas and plan future school meets.

---

## A Message to Future OBs

I have one simple piece of advice for all future OBs: **give it your all**.

Every effort you put in—no matter how small it may seem—can shape a child’s learning journey. A simple worksheet is not just a task; it’s an entry point into curiosity, understanding, and growth.

Your devotion, creativity, and passion leave an impact far beyond what you can see. Take the responsibility seriously, pour your heart into it, and remember this:

**Every child you teach carries a piece of your effort with them into their future.**
